### Google Scholar

Google Scholar search results include resources from our Library's online collections. Logging into your personal Google account will allow you to export citations to EndNote and other bibliographic management tools. For more information about searching our collections using Google Scholar and how to interpret search results, go to [Access information and tips](https://www.rmit.edu.au/library/borrowing-and-collections/collections/using-google-scholar "Australian library link on how to use Google Scholar. ") (links to the Australian library site).

Find free and editable resources published under open license. This link will take you to our RMIT library guides platform.

Check in with a librarian about what you need, we may be able to help! The library also purchases print and e-books to support teaching, learning and research. You can suggest a title to your Liaison Librarian by filling a form.
